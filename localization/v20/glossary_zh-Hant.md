# SpendZero glossary: Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant, Taiwan)

Written for Taiwan, readable in Hong Kong. Uses Taiwan vocabulary and Traditional characters. It is
not a character conversion of zh-Hans. Address the user as 你. Use full-width CJK punctuation and
「」 for quotes. Put a half-width space between CJK text and numbers or Latin words (連續 7 天, App 圖示).
Taiwan MOE usage: 紀錄 is the noun ("a record"), 記錄 is the verb ("to record").
